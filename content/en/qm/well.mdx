## Finite Square Well

A particle confined in a finite well of depth $V_0$ and width $L$ has a finite number of bound states whose wavefunctions leak into the classically forbidden region.

### Bound states

The bound-state energies satisfy the transcendental equations

$$
\tan\!\left(\tfrac{kL}{2}\right) = \frac{\kappa}{k} \quad (\text{even}), \qquad
\cot\!\left(\tfrac{kL}{2}\right) = -\frac{\kappa}{k} \quad (\text{odd})
$$

with $k = \sqrt{2mE}/\hbar$ and $\kappa = \sqrt{2m(V_0 - E)}/\hbar$.
